Blackjack rules

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their value, J, Q and K count 10, an ace counts 1 or 11. A blackjack (ace plus a ten-card as your first two cards) pays 3 to 2; other wins pay 1 to 1, a tie returns your bet. The dealer draws to 16 and stands on every 17. Double: double your bet for exactly one more card. Split: two cards of the same value become two hands with their own bet (once per round; split aces get one card each). Six decks, shuffled when a quarter is left. Keys: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, Space deal.
