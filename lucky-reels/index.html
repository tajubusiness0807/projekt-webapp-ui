At the table

    Roulette rules

    One wheel for everyone: every 45 seconds there are 30 seconds to place chips, then the ball is thrown and everybody sees the same number. Pick a chip, tap the table to put it down, then press Place bets (chips still waiting when the time runs out go in by themselves). You can place more until the time is up. European wheel with one zero. A single number pays 35 to 1. Red or black, odd or even, 1–18 or 19–36 pay 1 to 1. A dozen (1st, 2nd, 3rd 12) or a column (2:1 under the numbers) pays 2 to 1. Zero loses every outside bet. Winnings land on your balance as soon as the ball stops, even if you left the table.

    Pick a bet and drop a ball.

    Plinko rules

    Drop a ball from the top: at every row of pins it bounces left or right, and the slot it lands in multiplies your bet. The edges pay the most but are hit the least. Higher risk makes the edges bigger and the middle smaller; more rows give the ball more room to wander. Over the long run about 96% of bets come back. You can drop several balls at once. Space drops a ball.

    Pick a bet and help the chicken cross.

    Chicken rules

    Put down a bet and send the chicken across ten lanes of traffic, one hop at a time. Every lane it survives raises the multiplier; cash out whenever you like, or risk the next hop. Get hit and the bet is gone. Easy: about 1 in 12 hops ends badly, Medium about 1 in 6, Hard about 1 in 4, and the payouts grow to match. Over the long run about 96% of bets come back, whenever you cash out. Keys: Space hops, C cashes out.

        Horse racing rules

        Every three minutes six horses race, the same race for everyone. For two minutes you can bet on as many horses as you like (up to 12 bets per race). Win pays if your horse comes first; Place pays if it comes first or second, at lower odds. The odds are fixed when you bet: the favourite pays least, the outsiders most. When betting closes the gates open and everybody watches the race live; winnings land on your balance as soon as the race is over, even if you left the page. Over the long run about 92% of bets come back.

        On board

          Rocket rules

          One rocket for everyone. For 25 seconds you can board it with a bet (and, if you like, an automatic cash-out). Then it lifts off and the multiplier climbs faster and faster: about 2× after 5 seconds, 10× after 17 seconds. Cash out whenever you like and your bet pays that multiplier; if the rocket explodes first, the bet is gone. The higher it goes, the likelier the explosion: about 43% of rockets make it past 2×, one in eleven past 10×, and about one in eight blows up right on the pad. But one rocket in 300 is golden: you see it at liftoff, and it flies past 30× for sure. The few that reach 200× land on the moon and pay 200× to everyone still on board. Over the long run about 96% of bets come back if you hold every golden rocket to 30×. Space boards and cashes out.

          No-limit Hold'em · blinds 10/20

          Poker rules

          No-limit Texas Hold'em, blinds 10/20. Everyone gets two cards; five shared cards come in three rounds (flop, turn, river). Make the best five-card hand from your two cards and the board, or make everyone else fold. Each betting round you can fold, check or call, or raise (at least the last raise). Hands, best first: royal flush, straight flush, four of a kind, full house, flush, straight, three of a kind, two pair, pair, high card. Your buy-in comes from your credits; leaving the table puts your chips back. Online: up to 8 players share one table and one dealer, and you have 25 seconds per move; missing two moves in a row sits you out. Keys: F fold, C check/call, R raise.

          Blackjack rules

          Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Number cards count their value, J, Q and K count 10, an ace counts 1 or 11. A blackjack (ace plus a ten-card as your first two cards) pays 3 to 2; other wins pay 1 to 1, a tie returns your bet. The dealer draws to 16 and stands on every 17. Double: double your bet for exactly one more card. Split: two cards of the same value become two hands with their own bet (once per round; split aces get one card each). Six decks, shuffled when a quarter is left. Keys: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, Space deal.
